# Investment Guide — Grandparents → Grandchildren

> A practical, book-style guide to building and protecting wealth for minor grandchildren  
> using safe, government-backed Indian investment schemes.

---

## Purpose

This guide helps **grandparents** invest safely for their **minor grandchildren** over a 5–10 year horizon.  
It compares four government-backed options — SBI FD, NSC, Post Office TD, and SCSS — and recommends a balanced strategy that maximises growth while keeping operations simple.

All figures are based on **March 2026 rates**. Review rates at your bank or post office before investing.
