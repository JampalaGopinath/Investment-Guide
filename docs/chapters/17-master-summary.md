## Recommendation summary

!!! note "Side heading: Core takeaway"
    For minors' future corpus: **prioritise auto-compounding and low-effort structure**.  
    The winner is NSC (maximum growth) paired with SBI FD (maximum flexibility).

| Priority | Best choice |
|----------|-------------|
| Maximum corpus, no effort | NSC alone |
| Flexibility + growth balance | NSC + SBI FD (recommended split) |
| Regular quarterly income | SCSS |
| Moderate income with flexibility | PO TD (with reinvestment discipline) |
