# Chapter 16 — Final Recommended Plan

!!! note "Side heading: Recommendation"
    Split for balance: one **locked-growth leg** (NSC) + one **flexible leg** (SBI FD).  
    This gives you the best of both worlds with minimal ongoing effort.

    Total at Year 10 (assuming similar reinvestment rates): **~₹4,07,600**

## Implementation steps

1. **Gather all documents** — refer to [Chapter 10](10-documents.md) for the full checklist
2. **Open SBI FD** — visit a branch or use the YONO app (requires SBI account)
3. **Purchase NSC** — visit your nearest Post Office (Head Post Office is preferred)
4. **Set nominee and guardian** correctly on both instruments — refer to [Chapter 11](11-nominee-guardian.md)
5. **Scan / photograph** all receipts and certificates; store securely
6. **Set calendar reminders** for 1 week before each maturity date

!!! note "Side heading: Branch tip"
    For NSC, bring all documents and arrive early. Some post offices have limited staff for investment products. A **Head Post Office** is more reliable than a sub-post office for complex transactions.

## At maturity (Year 5)

1. Review current rates at SBI and Post Office
2. Decide whether to reinvest (NSC / FD / any new best product)
3. If grandchild is now 15–18, consider their imminent needs (education, etc.)
4. Reinvest the full maturity value to continue growth into Year 10 if not needed yet
