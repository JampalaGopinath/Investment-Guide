# Chapter 10 — Documents Checklist

!!! note "Side heading: Make it frictionless"
    Carry **originals and photocopies** of everything on your first visit.  
    Post offices and bank branches often ask for self-attested copies in addition to originals for verification.

## Grandparent (Investor)

- [ ] Aadhaar card (original + photocopy)
- [ ] PAN card (original + photocopy)
- [ ] Bank passbook — first page showing name + account number
- [ ] Address proof (if different from Aadhaar)
- [ ] 2 passport-size photos

## Grandchild (Minor beneficiary)

- [ ] Birth certificate (original + photocopy)
- [ ] Name + date of birth proof (school certificate, Aadhaar if available, attested)
- [ ] Address proof (if different from guardian)

## Parent / Guardian (if required to co-sign)

- [ ] Aadhaar card (original + photocopy)
- [ ] PAN card
- [ ] Relationship proof (birth certificate of child, if not self-evident)
- [ ] Bank passbook / cancelled cheque (if linked transfers are needed)

## At the branch / post office

- [ ] Completed application form (available at the branch)
- [ ] Nominee declaration form
- [ ] Guardian declaration form (for minor beneficiary)
- [ ] Cancelled cheque (bank) for electronic credit at maturity

!!! note "Side heading: Tip — photocopy in advance"
    Make 3–4 sets of photocopies before visiting. Some offices keep one set; others ask you to fill in separately each time. Having extras avoids a second trip.

## After completing the investment

- [ ] Collect the FD receipt / NSC certificate
- [ ] Scan or photograph it immediately
- [ ] Store the digital copy in a secure location (Google Drive, email to yourself, etc.)
- [ ] Note maturity date in phone calendar
