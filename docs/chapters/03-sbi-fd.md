# Chapter 3 — SBI FD (Best for Flexibility)

!!! note "Side heading: What it is"
    A cumulative FD compounds interest internally. You do not receive any payout until maturity. It can be broken early if needed (a penalty may apply).

## Product details

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Type** | Cumulative Term Deposit (STDR) |
| **Compounding** | Quarterly, stays inside FD |
| **Withdrawal** | Anytime (penalty if broken before maturity) |
| **Management** | Online via YONO app or branch |

## Current rates — Senior Citizens (March 2026)

| Tenure | Rate (p.a.) |
|--------|-------------|
| 1 year to < 2 years | 6.75% |
| 2 years to < 3 years | 6.95% |
| 3 years to < 5 years | 6.80% |
| **5 years to 10 years** | **7.05%** (includes We-care 0.50% premium) |

!!! note "Side heading: Senior citizen premium"
    SBI gives senior citizens an additional 0.50% over the standard rate on most tenures. The 5–10 year bucket currently benefits from this via the "We-care" offer.

## Advantages

- Flexible withdrawal with minimal penalty
- Can break and reinvest if rates rise in future
- Online management via YONO — no branch visits required
- Cumulative compounding (automatic "interest-on-interest" each quarter)
- Simple to open and operate

## Disadvantages

- Lower return than NSC (7.05% vs 7.7%)
- Premature withdrawal penalties reduce effective return
- Needs a periodic review if you want rate optimisation

## Example — ₹1,00,000 for 5 years

!!! note "Side heading: Worked example"
    At 7.05% p.a. compounded quarterly, ₹1,00,000 grows to approximately ₹1,41,800 after 5 years — giving ₹41,800 in interest.

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Principal | ₹1,00,000 |
| Rate | 7.05% p.a. |
| Tenure | 5 years |
| Maturity value | ₹1,41,800 (approx.) |
| Total interest | ₹41,800 |
