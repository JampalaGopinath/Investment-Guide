# Chapter 6 — Post Office TD "Loophole" (Critical)

!!! note "Side heading: The core issue"
    The TD principal does not increase — interest exits to savings every year.  
    This is not a bug, it is how the product is designed.  
    Understanding this is **critical** before choosing Post Office TD.

## The problem — simply put

Each year:

1. Your TD earns interest — e.g. ₹7,500 on ₹1,00,000 at 7.5%.
2. That ₹7,500 goes to your savings account.
3. **Next year, the TD is still ₹1,00,000** — not ₹1,07,500.
4. So you earn ₹7,500 again next year — not ₹7,500 × growth.

This means you are effectively earning **simple interest**, not compound interest, unless you actively reinvest the annual payout.

## What you must do to get compounding

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Interest credits to savings automatically (happens once a year) |
| 2 | Withdraw that interest from savings |
| 3 | Immediately reinvest it (new FD, RD, or TD) |

!!! note "Side heading: Effort required"
    This must be done **every year** for 5 years.  
    If you miss even one year, that interest sits in savings earning only ~4%.

## What happens if you don't reinvest

| Year | TD Interest Earned | Savings Balance (no reinvestment) | Lost Compounding |
|------|--------------------|-----------------------------------|-----------------|
| 1 | ₹7,500 | ₹7,500 | — |
| 2 | ₹7,500 | ₹15,000 (+ small 4% savings interest) | ~₹278 |
| 3 | ₹7,500 | ₹22,500 (approx.) | ~₹600 |
| 4 | ₹7,500 | ₹30,000 (approx.) | ~₹975 |
| 5 | ₹7,500 | ₹37,500 (approx.) | ~₹1,400 |
| **Total** | **₹37,500** | — | **~₹3,200+ lost** |

## Contrast with NSC

!!! note "Side heading: NSC comparison"
    NSC compounds internally — no action from you, no lost compounding.  
    This is the single biggest practical reason NSC outperforms Post Office TD despite a lower headline rate in some comparisons.

| Feature | Post Office TD (7.5%) | NSC (7.7%) |
|---------|-----------------------|------------|
| Annual payout | Yes (to savings) | No (stays locked) |
| Compounding | Manual (you must reinvest) | Automatic |
| Effort each year | High | None |
| 5-year value (₹1L) | ₹1,37,500 (no reinvest) / ₹1,43,630 (with reinvest) | ₹1,44,900 |
