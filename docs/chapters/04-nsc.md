# Chapter 4 — NSC (Best for Growth)

!!! note "Side heading: What it is"
    NSC (National Savings Certificate) is a 5-year locked government product. Interest compounds annually and is paid as a lump sum at maturity — no action required from you.

## Product details

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Type** | National Savings Certificate (5-year series) |
| **Lock-in** | 5 years (no early withdrawal allowed) |
| **Interest** | Compounded annually; paid at maturity |
| **Current rate** | 7.7% p.a. (Jan–Mar 2026 quarter) |
| **Backing** | Government of India |

!!! note "Side heading: Why it is truly 'set and forget'"
    Unlike Post Office TD or SCSS, NSC **does not pay out interest** into a separate account. The interest stays locked in the certificate and grows year after year automatically. You do nothing until maturity.

## Advantages

- Strong return for a fully safe product (7.7% compounding)
- True "set and forget" — no annual reinvestment steps
- Locked structure prevents impulsive withdrawal
- Government of India backing

## Disadvantages

- No liquidity for the full 5 years
- Cannot adjust if interest rates rise significantly
- Fixed 5-year cycle only; 10-year requires manual reinvestment after maturity

## Example — ₹1,00,000 for 5 years

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Principal | ₹1,00,000 |
| Rate | 7.7% p.a. compounded annually |
| Tenure | 5 years |
| Maturity value | ₹1,44,900 (approx.) |
| Total interest | ₹44,900 |

## 10-year illustration (requires reinvestment after Year 5)

!!! note "Side heading: 10-year assumption"
    NSC itself is only a 5-year product. A "10-year" outcome requires you to reinvest the maturity amount into a new NSC at whatever rate is available at that time.

| Stage | Value |
|-------|-------|
| After 5 years | ₹1,44,900 |
| Reinvest @ 7.7% (assumption) | ₹1,44,900 → ₹2,10,000 after another 5 years |
| Total interest (10 years) | ~₹1,10,000 |

*Note: The Year 6–10 rate is unknown today. The figure above is illustrative.*
