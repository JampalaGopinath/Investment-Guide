# Chapter 5 — Post Office Time Deposit (TD)

!!! note "Side heading: Important behaviour"
    Post Office TD credits interest **annually to a savings account** — so compounding is NOT automatic. You must reinvest the credited interest yourself to achieve true compounding returns.

## Product details

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Type** | Post Office Time Deposit (POTD) |
| **Lock-in** | None (early withdrawal allowed with penalty) |
| **Interest payout** | Credited annually to linked savings account |

## Current rates (March 2026)

| Tenure | Rate (p.a.) |
|--------|-------------|
| 1 year | 6.9% |
| 2 years | 7.0% |
| 3 years | 7.1% |
| **5 years** | **7.5%** |

## How it works step by step

1. You deposit ₹1,00,000 in a 5-year TD.
2. Each year, the Post Office calculates interest (calculated quarterly, credited annually).
3. That interest — e.g. ₹7,500 at 7.5% — lands in your linked savings account.
4. The TD principal remains ₹1,00,000 unchanged.
5. If you do nothing, the savings account earns ~4% on that interest money.
6. If you reinvest it manually (new FD / RD / TD), you can recover most of the compounding benefit.

!!! note "Side heading: The compounding gap"
    Because interest leaves the TD each year, the **effective compounded return without reinvestment is materially lower than the headline 7.5%**. See [Chapter 6](06-po-td-loophole.md) for the numbers.

## Advantages

- High 5-year rate (7.5%) — currently higher than SBI FD
- Government-backed (Post Office / India Post)
- Can access annual interest without breaking the TD
- Early withdrawal option available

## Disadvantages

- Annual payout breaks compounding by default
- Savings account earns only ~4% on the credited interest
- Requires discipline and effort to reinvest interest every year
- More operational effort than NSC

## Example — ₹1,00,000 for 5 years

!!! note "Side heading: Two outcomes"
    The gap between "do nothing" and "reinvest interest" is about ₹6,000 over 5 years — significant for a small corpus.

| Scenario | Maturity Value |
|----------|---------------|
| **Without reinvestment** | ₹1,37,500 (₹1,00,000 + ₹37,500 simple interest) |
| **With annual reinvestment of interest** | ₹1,43,630 (approx.) |
