# Chapter 1 — Goal & Scenario

!!! note "Side heading: Investment objective"
    Build a safe, government-backed corpus for minor grandchildren over 5–10 years with minimal manual effort.

## Investor profile

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Investor** | Grandparents (Senior Citizens) |
| **Investment amount** | ₹2,00,000 total (₹1,00,000 each) |
| **Time horizon** | 5–10 years |
| **Beneficiary** | Minor grandchildren |
| **Core need** | Safe growth + low maintenance |

## The scenario

Grandparents want to set aside money today so their grandchildren have a meaningful corpus when they need it — for education, a first step in life, or simply a head start.

!!! note "Side heading: Why government-backed?"
    Private market instruments carry credit or market risk.  
    Government-backed schemes (NSC, Post Office TD, SBI FD, SCSS) are considered virtually risk-free for small investors in India.

## Core requirements

- **Safety** — capital must not be at risk
- **Growth** — beat inflation over 5–10 years
- **Low maintenance** — grandparents should not need to actively manage the investment every month
- **Flexibility** — at least one leg should allow early access in an emergency
