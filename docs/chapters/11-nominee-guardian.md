# Chapter 11 — Nominee vs Guardian

!!! note "Side heading: Simple distinction"
    **Nominee** = receives funds after the investor dies.  
    **Guardian** = manages funds on behalf of a minor while the investor is alive.  
    These are two different roles. One person can fill both.

## Nominee

The nominee is the person who **receives the deposit** (or triggers the claim process) if the investor passes away before maturity.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Who can be nominee** | Spouse, child, grandchild, or any family member |
| **When active** | Only after the investor's death |
| **How to claim** | Death certificate + nominee KYC + claim form |
| **Receives** | Full maturity value (or current value at time of claim) |

!!! note "Side heading: Nominee is not an owner"
    A nominee is a **trustee**, not an owner. If the investor has a Will, the legal heirs named in the Will are the ultimate owners. Nominees typically hold the funds for the estate until the legal process is complete.

## Guardian

The guardian is the person who **operates the investment on behalf of a minor** while the investor is alive and the minor has not yet reached majority.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Who can be guardian** | Parent (natural guardian), or court-appointed guardian |
| **When active** | While the beneficiary is a minor (under 18) |
| **Role** | Signs forms, makes withdrawal decisions, receives maturity proceeds |

## Comparison table

| Aspect | Nominee | Guardian |
|--------|---------|----------|
| When active | After investor's death | During minor's years |
| Role | Receiver / trustee | Manager / decision-maker |
| Can be same person? | Yes (e.g. child's parent) | Yes |
| Required for minor? | Recommended | Often required |

!!! note "Side heading: Practical recommendation"
    Set the **grandchild's parent** as both **nominee** and **guardian** on all investments.  
    This keeps everything simple: one person handles both scenarios.
