# Chapter 12 — What Happens in a Death Case

!!! note "Side heading: Aim"
    A clearly named **nominee** + a **Will** reduces delays, disputes, and emotional hardship for the family.  
    Preparation takes an hour today and can save months of pain later.

## The process

| Step | Action | Who does it |
|------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Inform the bank / post office of the investor's passing | Nominee / family |
| 2 | Obtain the **death certificate** from the local municipal office | Family |
| 3 | Fill in the claim form at the bank / post office | Nominee |
| 4 | Submit claim with KYC documents of the nominee | Nominee |
| 5 | Bank / post office verifies and releases funds | Institution |

## Typical timelines

!!! note "Side heading: Speed depends on preparation"
    The single biggest factor affecting speed is whether a **valid nominee is registered** on the account.

| Scenario | Typical Time |
|----------|-------------|
| Nominee registered, all documents ready | 15–30 days |
| No nominee, but clear Will | 30–60 days |
| No nominee, no Will, disputed estate | Several months or longer |

## Documents required for claim

- Death certificate (original + copies)
- Nominee's Aadhaar + PAN
- Nominee's bank account details (for electronic transfer)
- Original FD receipt / NSC certificate (if available)
- Claim form (provided by branch/post office)

!!! note "Side heading: What if the certificate is lost?"
    For FDs: the bank can issue a duplicate on submitting an indemnity bond.  
    For NSC: the post office has a similar process for lost certificates.

## Recommendation

1. **Write a Will** — even a simple one witnessed and notarised.
2. Explicitly state which deposit is intended for which grandchild.
3. Nominate clearly — either the grandchild directly (if they will be 18 by maturity) or the parent as trustee.
4. Keep a copy of the Will with a trusted family member and mention it to the nominee.
